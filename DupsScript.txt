### The Core Logic (Module-by-Module)

* **Clean Names:** Removes numbers, spaces, and symbols, then converts names to lowercase.
* **Remove Trash:** Drops records where the cleaned name ends up completely empty.
* **Fix Blanks:** Standardizes all empty fields, `NaN`, and `NaT` to a blank string `''`.
* **Match Nicknames:** Groups short names with longer names if they are substrings and have matching or blank DOBs.
* **Separate Uniques:** Immediately splits completely unique names away from potential duplicate groups.
* **Group by DOB:** Generates a 6-digit Cluster ID using the matched name and DOB.
* **Fill Blank DOBs:** Maps blank DOBs to their matching parent cluster as a `Duplicate` (if 1 valid parent DOB) or `Potential Duplicate` (if 2+ conflicting parent DOBs).
* **Lock-In Scope:** Flags and locks records as **In-Scope** right after checking Name, DOB, and National ID—*before* any country split.
* **Split Countries:** Splits clusters into sub-clusters (e.g., `_1`, `_2`) if they contain different countries or IDs.
* **Preserve In-Scope:** Keeps split records in the final in-scope duplicates list even if the country split isolates them to a group size of 1.

* **Scenario 7:** Tests that true unique records bypass all matching and cleanly land in Out-Scope.
